    What each tab means. Share this page: #guide

    What this tool does

    Backtests walk-forward round projections (raw model μ at bet time) against pre-round DraftKings lines for completed tournaments. Data: round_projection_vs_actual_summary.csv (aggregates) and round_projection_vs_actual.csv (every player × round × market). Regenerate with npm run export:round-projection-vs-actual.

    Toolbar filters

    Tournament
    One event, or all combined (each event uses its latest export).
    Market
    Total score, Birdies, Bogeys, GIR, Fairways, or all.
    Min EV %
    Global floor; GIR 10%, Total score, Birdies & Bogeys 15% per-market minimum. Default 12.5%.
    Bet side
    Over only, under only, or both.
    Player
    Name search (Bet log & Risk).
    Show
    Bet log only: qualified bets vs all graded lines (every DK line with a result).

    Overview

    High-level dashboard: calibration + betting edge at a glance.

    • Total score RMSE / MAE — How far projected round scores sit from DK lines (lower = closer).
    • EV units / ROI — Flat 1-unit P/L on every side clearing min EV %.
    • Best edge pocket — Market + side with best ROI at your filter.
    • Charts: RMSE by market; EV ROI by market.

    Line accuracy

    Model line vs DK line before the round — not win rate.

    • RMSE — Root mean square error (model − DK). Lower is better.
    • MAE — Mean absolute error.
    • Line pairs — Count of player-round-markets with both lines.

    EV backtest

    Aggregated betting if you bet every qualifying side at flat 1 unit on pre-round DK odds.

    • Table: market × EV threshold × side → bets, W–L–P, units, ROI, win %.
    • Heatmap — Best ROI per market/threshold cell (green = +, red = −).

    Best bets

    Live-week action list for the upcoming round in projections.json (not graded backtest rows).

    • Model μ — Full projection mean: course SG emphasis, venue history, 8–12 round hot/cold form, birdie-heavy venue boosts, weather, morning/afternoon tee wave, and in-round scratch when live.
    • Δ — Model μ minus the DraftKings line (positive = model above the book).
    • EV — Model edge vs posted DraftKings odds (Poisson birdies, binomial GIR/FW, normal score).
    • Context — Course fit tags (dominant SG, recent form), weather, tee wave, and historical signal buckets.
    • Score — EV × OOS market factor × context boost (ranking only).
    • Refresh live data: npm run refresh:live then reload this page.

    Bet log

    One row = player × round × market (pre-round DK audit). Model column is walk-forward μ — not book-calibrated.

    Model / DK / Δ
    Walk-forward projection, DK closing line, and difference.
    Over / Under
    American odds and W/L vs actual for each side.
    Pick / EV% / Fair EV%
    Bet side (≥ min EV), edge vs posted odds, edge vs devigged true price (no margin).
    Bet / P/L
    Result and flat 1u profit on your pick.

    KPIs: beat fair price (hit rate − fair implied), DK margin, model vs fair.

    Risk

    Dollar simulation and stake sizing from your bankroll.

    • Flat 1 unit (fixed) — Same $ every bet (1% of starting bankroll).
    • Flat 1% (compound) — 1% of current bankroll each bet.
    • Quarter / Half Kelly — Kelly sizing with caps; +1% cap matches main +EV app.
    • Round exposure cap — Max total staked per event-round.
    • Outputs: ending bankroll, drawdown, bankroll curve, suggested stake by market, recent bets in $.

    All tournaments

    Card per event: score RMSE/MAE, EV units & ROI on all markets at your min edge (walk-forward model vs pre-round DK). Click a card to filter.

    odds.csv

    Long-horizon backtest on every graded DK line in odds.csv (Birdies & Total score). Walk-forward model μ vs opening/closing odds.

    • Filters — Course dropdown plus toolbar Market / Min EV % / Bet side / Player.
    • Model picks only — Keep sides the model would bet at your EV threshold (μ vs line + edge).
    • Group by — Roll up hit %, ROI, and P/L by market, course, or player.
    • Regenerate: npm run backtest:odds-model-roi

    Insights

    Auto bullets from current filters: calibration notes, best/worst pockets, beat-fair summary.

    Assumptions

    • Pre-round DK lines/odds only (pre_round_audit).
    • Default pricing mode/skill unless noted.
    • Flat 1u in Overview, EV backtest, Bet log; Risk tab uses your bankroll rules.
