    Select Devig Books

    Choose which sportsbooks set the fair probability for EV, Kelly, and CS. For head-to-head markets you can also pick a method that changes how tight the fair price sits versus the posted lines. Apply & rescan updates the +EV table.

    Picking a method

    Methods only affect two-way matchups (not win/top-N outrights). They change how the app turns posted odds into a single fair price for EV, Kelly, and CS.

    Tip: Start with Multiplicative or Average. Use Worst case when you want the stingiest fair price for your side. If unsure, compare a couple of methods and see which CS lines up with how you read the market.

    Default

    100% weight

    One book carries full consensus weight.

    Split weights

    Apply saves preferences and rescans the +EV table.

    +EV Bets

    Scan matchup and outright bets in one place, sorted by Model EV so the biggest model edges float to the top. Narrow the list with the filters, then compare model prices to book prices column by column.

    Weather

    Use the same weather controls as Round projections and Hole Hangout. Set temp, wind, humidity, and Condition to match the expected day; that updates fair win chances on every row here. Choosing Default for condition resets the numbers to the baseline weather snapshot (then adjust sliders again if needed).

    Pricing

    Mode and Skill match the rest of the app: switch them when the goal is to weight recent form, this course, or a specific part of the game. Model % and Model EV update to reflect that choice.

    Toolbar

    Golfer
    Limit the table to one player.
    Market
    Show only one product type (for example round matchups, tournament matchups, win, top 10).
    Best books
    Pick one sportsbook to keep only rows where the best price for that bet is there, or All books to show every book.
    Max odds
    Hide longshots at that American price or longer. The default 10000 keeps odds below +10000.
    Bankroll ($)
    Your bankroll for the quarter Kelly dollar suggestion (subject to the app’s stake caps).
    Boost / Boost %
    Turn on when a promo boosts profit on winning bets; Model EV and displayed odds follow the boost.
    Devig
    Opens book selection, weights, and devig method for EV, Kelly, and the CS column. Tap Apply & rescan after changes.

    Table columns

    Model EV
    Edge vs the best available price at a glance: green for positive, red for negative.
    Kelly
    Suggested quarter Kelly stake in dollars from the bankroll above, using the boosted best price.
    Best book
    Where that best price lives; American odds (boosted when a boost is on).
    Golfer
    Who the bet is on.
    Model
    American odds implied by the model win chance (no sportsbook margin).
    CS
    Consensus “market fair” odds built from the books and weights in Devig. Use it to see how the model compares to the books you care about.
    Implied
    Win probability implied by the actual best price (after boost).
    Δ (delta)
    How far the model win % sits above or below that implied win %.
    Market
    Whether the row is a matchup type or an outright market.
    Bet
    The exact matchup wording or outright label.

    Devig settings

    Open Devig to choose which books feed EV, Kelly, and CS, split weights between them, and (for head-to-heads) pick a method that nudges consensus tighter or looser versus the board. Selected devig books are hidden from the bet offers so you do not bet into the same book used as the fair source.

    Tip: Try Average or Multiplicative first. Use Worst case when you want a cautious read on an edge.

    Round projections

    For the Round you pick (R1–R4), each row is one golfer, one DraftKings-scraped market (only stats returned by the live DK props run are listed), and either Over or Under on that line. Columns show the model projection in Proj, Market rating and Course rating (each on a 1–100 scale; 50 ≈ average), the book line, a Book mark (DraftKings logo), the price for that side, model probability at the line, and edge vs the book. The table loads sorted by Edge (highest first). Click a row to expand a detail strip: discrete outcome distribution (line vs over), projection snapshot, and model inputs. Tap the same row again to collapse. Markets follow the same weather and pricing adjustments as the rest of the app. Hover a row for mean and σ used on the model side of the line.

    Table

    Tap a column header to sort. On load, rows are ordered by Edge (highest first). When two rows tie on the active sort column, order falls back to golfer, then market, then Over before Under. Use Golfer and Market to limit rows. Only DraftKings lines and odds for the selected round are shown; golfers without a DK line for that market are omitted. Each golfer–market pair with DK odds shows Over and Under. Changing Round or either filter collapses any expanded row.

    Weather

    Dial in temp, wind, humidity, and Condition to match the forecast. Default condition resets the sliders to the baseline snapshot; then nudge again for a custom day. These controls update fair numbers together with Pricing.

    Market & course ratings

    Market rating (1–100)
    How this golfer’s recent rounds on this stat compare to PGA Tour averages (same window as projections benchmarks). 50 ≈ tour average; higher = more birdies, GIR, etc. (or lower scores on round score).
    Course rating (1–100)
    How this week’s course compares to all PGA venues on the same stat (venue historical average vs the average across every course with enough rounds in the benchmark window). Hover for venue avg vs all-courses avg. 50 ≈ typical course on the 1–100 scale.

    Pricing

    How to use it
    Default mode locks Skill to Default as well (no pillar tilt). Recent form favors players trending up or down. Course history favors players with a strong record at this event. Skill focus lets you choose a spelled-out pillar (Total SG, Off the tee, Approach, Around the green, Putting, Tee to green). The same Mode and Skill row appears anywhere Pricing is shown.

    Historical Trends

    Compare past rounds to the model line for one golfer and stat. The header shows who and which event. Filters hide rounds that do not match typed weather values (Temp °F uses optional min and max — leave either blank for no bound; wind mph and humidity % still map to bands — leave blank for all), the Course dropdown (when not All), or optional Current course only (this week’s venue from projections).

    How to use the sidebar

    Golfer and market
    Select who to study and which stat the bars represent.
    Rounds and line
    Under the chart, set how many recent rounds to include and which Line to use for over or under hit rates and the small EV readout.
    Hit rates and top table
    See over and under frequency in the window, then scroll the league table for the same line.

    Live Prop Predictor

    Estimate full-round totals mid-event using the same stat columns as Round projections, plus how uncertain the remaining holes are from historical rounds (when hole-by-hole rows exist) and the usual Round projection spread scaled to holes left.

    How to fill it in

    Through … holes
    How many holes are finished (0–18), in order from Starting hole (e.g. start 10 → holes 10–18 then 1–9). Round score “to par” uses each hole’s par from the current course.
    Starting hole
    Shotgun or split-tee order: the first hole you played this round (1 or 10, etc.). Historical checkpoints use the same order when hole-by-hole data exists.
    Fairways hit
    Par 3s are not fairway holes; remainder mean and volatility use only par 4/5 holes from projections.json hole pars for this venue.
    Round score, birdies, pars, bogeys
    Where history includes hole-by-hole scores for a round, we learn the distribution of “what’s left” after your checkpoint and blend it with the model.
    GIR, fairways, putts
    Hole files rarely break these down per hole; we scale the Round projection mean and volatility to the holes remaining (fairways: par 3s excluded when course hole pars exist).
    Weather & pricing
    Uses the same global Weather and Pricing controls as the Round projections tab — adjust there first.
    Live line & prices
    When projections.json includes DraftKings round props, changing golfer or market fills DK line and Over/Under prices; otherwise defaults apply and you can edit.

    Matchup Analysis Tool

    What each column header means on this tab.

    Prices & edge

    Golfer name
    Column for that side of the matchup.
    Model
    Estimated win probability for that golfer.
    Market
    Implied win probability from the posted prices.
    Edge
    Expected value vs fair at the price shown (positive is favorable).
    Odds
    American odds for that golfer at the listed book.
    Best Book
    Best decimal price among the same sportsbooks used on the +EV tab (respects your Devig book toggles).

    Strokes gained vs field

    Shown for head-to-head markets only. The section is hidden for 3-balls.

    Metric
    Stat being compared on that row.
    Golfer columns
    That golfer’s value for the metric, with a bar for scale.
    Field %
    Share of the field with a strictly lower value on that metric (higher is better).
    Advantage
    Which golfer leads and by how much on that row.

    Metric definitions

    SG: Total
    Overall DataGolf skill rating in strokes gained per round versus the field.
    SG: Tee-to-Green
    Combined non-putting skill: off the tee, approach, and around the green.
    SG: Off the Tee
    Driving skill after tee shots, including distance, accuracy, and position gained.
    SG: Approach
    Iron and approach-shot skill after the tee shot before short game or putting.
    SG: Around Green
    Short-game skill near the green, excluding putting.
    SG: Putting
    Putting skill measured as strokes gained on the greens.
    Driving distance rating
    DataGolf yards vs an average PGA Tour field (from skill-ratings); when live measured drives exist, the table can show absolute yards instead.
    Accuracy rating
    DataGolf skill-rating driving accuracy value, shown as points versus the field baseline.

    Round Matchups

    Browse head-to-head and three-ball markets. Each row lists both players, a model win percentage, estimated EV, and the best posted price on the board.

    Market and round

    Use Market to switch tournament, single-round, or three-ball lists. Set Round on the Round projections tab first; this table follows that choice. Adjust Weather and Pricing on other tabs to update fair win chances here.

    Pricing

    The Pricing row mirrors the rest of the app: pick a mode when the read should lean on form, this course, or a skill pillar.

    Outrights

    Choose a market (win, places, cut, and so on), then compare the Model column to each book. Tap headers to sort. Use Refresh projections after updating odds outside the app so the table matches the latest file.

    Weather and pricing

    Weather lives on Round projections, +EV, or Hole Hangout but still moves fair chances here. Use the Pricing row to align with form, course, or skill the same way as on other tabs.

    Hole Hangout

    The course under the tabs applies to every screen. Pick Hole and Player (field follows the round on Round projections). Results refresh when you change weather, pricing, hole, player, or live scenario; Run simulation forces an immediate refresh. The same settings reproduce the same sample shot path.

    Live inputs

    Turn on Live inputs to set lie, yards to the hole, which shot it is, and putt length. Use it to mimic standing in a specific spot instead of a generic tee shot.

    Weather

    The Weather line shows the same morning / afternoon forecast summary as Round projections (venue hourly feed + tee waves).

    Pricing

    Pricing mode shifts how bullish the sim is on birdies versus mistakes, using the same Mode and Skill as everywhere else.

    Course fit

    This tab compares how players match the current field and how much this week’s venue profile stresses different parts of the game. Use it when you care whether someone’s strengths line up with what the track tends to reward or punish.

    Two views

    Adjustments — Radar chart, course similarity, and the course ratings table (full field). Predicted shot distance — A grid of how many shots per round the model expects in each distance bucket (putting, rough approach, fairway approach).

    Adjustments

    Golfer selector
    Pick a golfer to overlay their profile on the radar. With no selection you still see how the venue curve differs from the field.
    Course filter
    Choose which venue’s historical profile drives the green venue curve and the similarity list; it defaults to this week’s course from projections.
    Radar
    Each spoke is a skill area (Driving Accuracy, Off Tee, Approach, Around Green, Putting, Driving Distance). The shapes show Field Avg (dashed baseline), the selected course profile (green), an optional comparison course from the similarity list (blue), and your selected golfer (gold). The chart uses normalized profiles (0–1), not strokes gained.
    Course similarity
    A ranked list of other venues whose historical shape is closest to this one. Click a row to highlight it and draw that course’s profile on the radar; click again to clear.

    Course ratings table

    Lists every player in the current tournament field. All numeric cells are course-fit ratings (field-relative scores). They are not strokes gained and are not the same units as SG columns elsewhere in the app.

    Golfer
    Name only. Use the search box to filter.
    Drv Acc, Off Tee, Approach, Arg, Putt, Distance
    One column per radar spoke. Each value is that player’s skill vs the field on that dimension (z-scored across everyone in the table). 0 ≈ average; positive = stronger than the field on that spoke; negative = weaker. These are not strokes gained. See Course rating for overall fit to this venue’s radar profile.
    Course rating
    Overall fit to this venue: each spoke’s field z-score is weighted by that spoke’s demand on the green radar profile (Approach uses iron-play demand, not raw ARG splits), then z-scored across the field. Higher = skills that matter most here, relative to everyone else. Round projections still use the separate course_table SG×coefficient adjustment.
    Winner, Top 5, Top 10, Top 20
    Best available sportsbook outright prices for that player (separate from the rating columns).
    Sort
    Click any column header to sort. Green/red coloring only highlights positive vs negative ratings, not EV or SG.

    Predicted shot dist'n

    Columns
    Putting splits expected putts into short, medium, and long bands from the same putting picture as the rest of the app. Rough and fairway approach split approach shots by lie and distance; counts are shown per round and lined up against the field average for each bin.
    Numbers and bars
    Each cell is predicted shots per round in that bin. The faint marker is the field baseline for the bin; the colored dot is this golfer. Green versus red indicates whether their underlying skill in that bucket is above or below the field for rough and fairway rows; putting uses putting skill vs the field.
    Hover a cell
    You get the exact predicted count versus the field baseline for that bin, a compact comparison strip, and a related headline stat (model putts, fairways, or greens in regulation vs the field). When an over/under line and prices are available for that stat, they appear at the bottom; otherwise that line is omitted.
